# Combat sound bank

Original project audio rendered by `tools/build_combat_audio.py`. CC0-1.0. No third-party samples are included. These are designed/synthesized assets, not recorded human foley. The generator, seed, hashes, loop flags, frames, RMS and peaks are recorded for reproducibility.

Three variations per family/phase; runtime playback uses the WAV bank instead of synthesizing six cues. Listening review in the game is required; sample counts or tests do not establish aesthetic quality.
